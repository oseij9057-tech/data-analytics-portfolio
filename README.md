## Projects

### Lunex Data: From Raw Order Logs to a Live Business Dashboard

Built a live business intelligence solution for a Ghanaian mobile data reselling business by transforming 200+ fragmented order records into a structured dataset and interactive Power BI dashboard.

**Tools:** Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX

**Key Highlights:**
- Reconstructed, cleaned, and deduplicated 200+ transactions.
- Corrected data-entry and record-alignment issues.
- Built DAX measures for sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Connected Excel to Power BI through OneDrive for ongoing data refresh.
- Identified network performance, bundle popularity, pending orders, and monthly sales trends.

### Sales & Stock Analysis: Import & Reselling Business

Analyzed sales and stock data for a Ghanaian import and reselling business dealing in electronics, appliances, bikes, and footwear.

**Tools:** Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX

**Key Highlights:**
- Restructured a manually maintained dataset with significant data-quality issues.
- Standardized 45 raw item descriptions into 12 categories.
- Built a live dashboard tracking revenue, payment status, outstanding balances, categories, and shipments.
- Identified missing payment records, unrecorded prices, and significant outstanding revenue.
- Delivered a cleaned dataset, dashboard, written report, and recommendations.

### B2B Supply Chain Analytics

### China–Ghana Trade Analysis

### FreshDrop Logistics Analysis

### Toothpaste Preference Survey
